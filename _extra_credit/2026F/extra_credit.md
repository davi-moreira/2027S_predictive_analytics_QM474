> Only courses **completed during this term (after August 24, 2026)** are eligible; **partially finished courses, even those with substantial progress, will not be awarded points**.

>

## Why DataCamp needs to be announced early

The spacing rule makes late awareness unrecoverable. Five completions, none sharing a date and none
within two days of another, needs a **minimum of roughly 13 days** of elapsed calendar time even if a
student does nothing else, and realistically far more since each course takes hours of work. With a
Dec 4 deadline and eligibility starting Aug 24, a student who first hears about DataCamp in November
cannot earn the full 5% no matter how hard they work.

This is the strongest argument for publishing the extra-credit rules in the first weeks of the term
rather than leaving them only in the syllabus document.
