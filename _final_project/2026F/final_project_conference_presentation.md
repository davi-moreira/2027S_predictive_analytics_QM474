# Final Project — Conference Poster Presentation

## About the Final Project

The Final Project is a **group capstone** culminating in a **research poster** presented at the Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference. The project is worth **35%** of the course grade: Milestone Deliverables 30%, Peer Evaluation 20%, Peer Review 10%, **Poster Presentation at the conference 20%**, Instructor/TA Evaluation 20%.

---

## Instructions

As a proof of your Poster Presentation, you must **upload a photo of yourself in front of your poster**.

---

## Context

**Presentation at the Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference is required for all students.** The final poster is due one week before the conference, after which posters are printed and distributed during a dedicated Poster Presentation Preparation class.

As the event may not coincide with our regular class time, please communicate with your other course instructors in advance regarding potential scheduling conflicts. If any issues arise, please let Professor Moreira know. We will not hold our usual class immediately following the Poster Presentation, allowing you time to rest and catch up on other coursework. Consult the course schedule for further details.

Conference information: <https://www.purdue.edu/undergrad-research/conferences/index.php>

## What to Submit on Brightspace

## Tips

- **Take the photo at the conference**, with the poster visible behind you.
- **Take it early in your session.** Do not leave it until the room is clearing.
- Make sure the poster is legible enough to identify as yours.
