# Final Project — Peer Review Submission

## About the Final Project

The Final Project is a **group capstone** culminating in a **research poster** presented at the Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference. The project is worth **35%** of the course grade: Milestone Deliverables 30%, Peer Evaluation 20%, **Peer Review 10%**, Poster Presentation at the conference 20%, Instructor/TA Evaluation 20%.

> **Peer Review is not Peer Evaluation.** Peer *Review* (this assignment, 10%) is your group reviewing **other groups' posters**. Peer *Evaluation* (20%, submitted at the end of the course) is each member confidentially rating **their own teammates' contributions**. Both exist, they are graded separately, and they are submitted at different times.

---

## Overview

In this assignment, each group will critically evaluate the assigned peers' Final Posters by reviewing PDF files of their work and completing a structured online survey. Your goal is to provide constructive, actionable feedback that will help your classmates improve their research and presentation skills.

## Instructions

### 1. Access the Posters

Check the posters your group must review in the table at the end of these instructions.

A shared folder with the posters has been provided at: **[Dropbox Folder Link]**

Download or view each group's poster PDF in this folder.

### 2. Review Each Poster Thoroughly

Pay attention to the research question, methods, findings, and visual design of each poster.

Take notes as you go along; these will be helpful when filling out the survey.

### 3. Complete the Poster Peer Review Survey

Open the survey: **[link]**

Fill out **one survey per poster** that you evaluate.

Take a **screenshot confirming each submission**.

Provide constructive written comments where prompted. Remember, thoughtful feedback is more helpful than general praise or criticism.

### 4. Submission Requirements

Submit a **.PDF file containing your screenshots** (one for each survey submission).

Ensure all surveys are completed before the deadline.

---

## Tips for Effective Feedback

**Be Specific:** Instead of "I didn't like the plots," try "It would help to label the X-axis more clearly in the plot about ...".

**Stay Constructive:** Focus on improvement: "Consider adding a short paragraph explaining your data cleaning process."

**Balance Positives and Negatives:** Recognize what they did well, while also pointing out areas for enhancement.
