# Milestone 04 — Instructor and TA Meetings (Round 01 Confirmation)

## About the Final Project

The Final Project is a **group capstone** culminating in a **research poster** presented at the Fall 2026 Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference. Across the term your group completes milestones **M00 through M13** (M00 through M13, with no gaps in the numbering). The project is worth **35%** of the course grade: Milestone Deliverables 30%, Peer Evaluation 20%, Peer Review 10%, Poster Presentation at the conference 20%, Instructor/TA Evaluation 20%.

## Instructions

Please upload a PDF document detailing the dates and times of your group meetings with me and the TAs.

## Context

This milestone closes the loop opened in [Milestone 00](milestone_00_meetings_schedule_and_group_contract.md), where your group emailed the TA and Professor Moreira to propose times. M00 submitted the **requests**; M04 confirms the meetings actually **happened**.

Round 01 meetings take place between **Mon Sep 21 and Sun Oct 11**: one with the course TA, one with Professor Moreira. At least one member of your group must attend each meeting, and it does not have to be the same member both times.
