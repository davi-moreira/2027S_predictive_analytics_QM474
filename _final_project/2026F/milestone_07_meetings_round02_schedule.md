# Milestone 07 — Instructor and TA Meetings (Round 02 Schedule)

## About the Final Project

The Final Project is a **group capstone** culminating in a **research poster** presented at the Fall 2026 Purdue Undergraduate Research Conference. Across the term your group completes milestones **M00 through M13** (M00 through M13, with no gaps in the numbering). The project is worth **35%** of the course grade: Milestone Deliverables 30%, Peer Evaluation 20%, Peer Review 10%, Poster Presentation at the conference 20%, Instructor/TA Evaluation 20%.

---

## Assignment Instructions

In this second round of meetings, each group is required to schedule the following:

**One meeting with the course TA:** It must take place between **October 19 and November 1**.

**One meeting with me:** This must take place between **November 2 and November 7**.

At least one member of your group must attend the meetings; there is no need to be the same member in all the meetings.

The purpose of these meetings is to discuss your project and its development. We will focus on your predictive models, predictive modeling strategy, and poster development.

## Submission Requirement

You must upload **two PDF files** containing copies of the emails sent to both the TA and me to schedule these meetings. Each email must:

- Include your **proposed dates and times**.
- **Specify your course section.**
- **Copy all group members.**
