## What this milestone is for

This milestone closes the loop you opened in Milestone 00. There your group emailed the TA and Professor Moreira to propose meeting times; here you **present your project at both Round 01 meetings** and confirm that the meetings took place. Presenting forces the group to agree on where the project stands, and it lets the TA and Professor Moreira spend the meeting on your real problems instead of on catching up.

## What to do

Hold your two Round 01 meetings:

- the **TA meeting**, between Monday, February 8 and Sunday, February 21;
- the **meeting with Professor Moreira**, between Monday, February 22 and Sunday, February 28.

These meetings focus on your prediction problem, your data and your proposed modeling strategy. **Every member attends at least one of the two meetings and presents part of the deck**; it does not have to be the same people both times.

**Every meeting starts with your group's presentation.** Prepare about five slides and five minutes:
(1) the stakeholder and the decision your predictions support; (2) your data; (3) what you did since the
last meeting; (4) your current results, in the course's terms; (5) the two or three questions you want
help with. Update the deck between the TA meeting and the meeting with Professor Moreira, so the second
one shows what changed after the first.

Upload the slides to this milestone's Brightspace folder **before** each meeting: `NN_round01_ta_slides.pdf` before the TA meeting and `NN_round01_instructor_slides.pdf` before the meeting with Professor Moreira. The folder opens on Monday, February 8 and accepts several uploads.

After both meetings, make one short PDF that lists, for each of the two meetings, whether it was with the TA or with Professor Moreira, its date and time, who from your group attended and who presented.

## What to submit

One member uploads, for the whole group, the two slide decks (each before its meeting) and `NN_meetings_round01.pdf` (both meetings in the same file), for example `03_round01_ta_slides.pdf`, `03_round01_instructor_slides.pdf` and `03_meetings_round01.pdf`.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: both slide decks, each uploaded before its meeting and presented there, and the confirmation PDF by the deadline, with the date, the time, the attendees and the presenters of both meetings. **A meeting held without a presentation does not count**, and a member who presents at neither meeting does not receive this milestone's credit.

## Tips

- Rehearse once. Five minutes goes fast; the questions slide is the one that gets cut, and it is the most useful.
- Write down the date, the time and who attended right after each meeting, not from memory weeks later.
- If different members went to each meeting, say so.
- The meeting with Professor Moreira can fall late in its window, close to the deadline: upload once it has happened.

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
