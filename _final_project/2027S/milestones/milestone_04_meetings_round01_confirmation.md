## What this milestone is for

This milestone closes the loop you opened in Milestone 00. There your group emailed the TA and Professor Moreira to propose meeting times; here you confirm that both Round 01 meetings actually took place.

## What to do

Hold your two Round 01 meetings:

- the **TA meeting**, between Monday, February 8 and Sunday, February 21;
- the **meeting with Professor Moreira**, between Monday, February 22 and Sunday, February 28.

At least one member of your group attends each meeting, and it does not have to be the same person both times. These meetings focus on your prediction problem, your data and your proposed modeling strategy.

Then make one short PDF that lists, for each of the two meetings, whether it was with the TA or with Professor Moreira, its date and time, and who from your group attended.

## What to submit

One member uploads `NN_meetings_round01.pdf` for the whole group, for example `03_meetings_round01.pdf`. Both meetings go in the same file.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: one PDF, by the deadline, with the date, the time and the attendees of both meetings.

## Tips

- Write down the date, the time and who attended right after each meeting, not from memory weeks later.
- If different members went to each meeting, say so.
- The meeting with Professor Moreira can fall late in its window, close to the deadline: upload once it has happened.

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
