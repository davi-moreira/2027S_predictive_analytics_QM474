## What this milestone is for

Presenting your poster at the **Spring Undergraduate Research Conference** is required for everyone in the course; the poster session is on **Tuesday, April 13, 2027**. To present, your group must apply. Applications go through OURConnect, whose abstract deadline is Monday, March 1, at 11:59 PM. This milestone is due one day earlier, so a login problem or a missing field does not cost you your place.

## What to do

Submit one application per group in OURConnect (you reach it from the conference page, <https://www.purdue.edu/undergrad-research/conferences/index.php>), and follow the official abstract submission instructions (attached on Brightspace). Enter:

- **Title:** your project title.
- **Abstract:** your abstract from Milestone 03, improved with any feedback you have. Do not put author names inside the abstract box.
- **Can the abstract be published in the booklet?** Yes.
- **Keywords:** five keywords that identify your project.
- **Authors:** every group member.
- **Format:** in person only.
- **Availability on the conference day:** select multiple options.
- **Presentation type:** you would *not* like to be considered for a research talk.
- **Primary research category:** Business Case Study.
- **Final judging unit:** Daniels School of Business.
- **Digital Humanities, Data Stewardship, LLLC, Celebrate:** No.
- **Mentor:** Davi Cordeiro Moreira, `dcordeir@purdue.edu`. Use this official Purdue email exactly.

After you apply, you can still edit your abstract until **Monday, March 22**, when final edits close. Professor Moreira then approves the abstract as your mentor by Wednesday, March 24.

## What to submit

One member uploads `NN_conference_application.pdf` for the whole group: a screenshot of the confirmation page or of the confirmation email, saved as a PDF.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: one PDF, by the deadline, that shows your group's application was submitted.

## Tips

- Apply early in the window. Accounts, co-author entries and the abstract box all take longer than expected.
- If the form words a field slightly differently from this list, choose the closest option.
- If anything blocks your registration, tell Professor Moreira right away, not on the deadline.

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
