## What this milestone is for

Your second round of meetings comes when your models are taking shape and the poster is next. The conversation moves from the data to your **predictive models, your modeling strategy and your poster**. This milestone is where you ask for those meetings.

## What to do

Send two emails, one to the TA and one to Professor Moreira, asking for a meeting:

- the **TA meeting** takes place between Monday, March 8 and Sunday, March 14, or between Monday, March 22 and Sunday, March 28 (there are no meetings during spring break, March 15 to March 20);
- the **meeting with Professor Moreira** takes place between Monday, March 29 and Saturday, April 3.

Each email offers several concrete dates and times, names your course section, and copies every group member. At least one member attends each meeting, and it does not have to be the same person both times.

The meeting with Professor Moreira comes just before your final poster is due (Milestone 10, Sunday, April 4), so bring your poster draft and its open questions. You confirm that both meetings took place in Milestone 13.

## What to submit

One member uploads two PDFs for the whole group, each a copy of an email as sent: `NN_ta_meeting_request_round02.pdf` and `NN_instructor_meeting_request_round02.pdf`. Replace NN with your group number.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: both files, by the deadline, each email showing the proposed times, your section and all members copied.

## Tips

- Send both emails well before the deadline. People's calendars fill up before spring break.
- Offer three or four real slots per email rather than asking when the recipient is free.
- Put your section number in both emails, and copy every group member.

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
