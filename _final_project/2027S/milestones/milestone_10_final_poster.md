## What this milestone is for

This is the poster you present at the Spring Undergraduate Research Conference on Tuesday, April 13. It is due nine days before the conference, which leaves time for printing and for your presentation planning (Milestones 11 and 12). If you follow these instructions, Professor Moreira prints your poster for free, and it is handed out in a poster preparation class before the conference.

## What to do

1. **Start from your feedback.** Reread the rubric (attached on Brightspace), notebook 17, and the feedback on your first draft (Milestone 09), and fix what it flagged.
2. **Check the evidence.** Every claim comes from cross-validation on the training set (Milestones 06 and 08); the test set was opened once, for the champion only, and never used to choose. Make your stakeholder's decision and your champion easy to find.
3. **Model comparison (rubric criterion 6).** State the primary metric and your margin δ, with what it means for your stakeholder, and when you set it. Give each model's fold scores, mean and standard deviation. Compare the baseline and the tuned model (and any other finalist) on the same five folds: the per-fold differences, their mean, the interval corrected for overlapping folds, and the outcome (worthwhile gain, worthwhile loss, practically equivalent, unresolved). Whether two models' separate results overlap does not decide the comparison. With more than two candidates, show the comparisons that led to your champion.
4. **Champion (rubric criterion 6).** Name one champion and justify it in a short statement, in your stakeholder's terms, that covers four points: what you recommend and for which decision; the evidence (the outcome, the mean gain, its interval and δ); the checks (secondary metrics, fit time, interpretability) and the runner-up; and what would change your mind. A more complex model replaces the baseline only on a worthwhile gain; otherwise keep the simpler model and say which outcome it was. The baseline can win.
5. **Check the required sections.** The Acknowledgments and the AI Use Disclosure are both on the poster, and nothing on it identifies a person.

## What to submit

One member uploads a single PDF for the whole group, named with your group number only: group 01 uploads `01.pdf`, group 17 uploads `17.pdf`. Do not add your section number.
