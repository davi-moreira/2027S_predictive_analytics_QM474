## What this milestone is for

A poster session is a chance to show your work to the people who matter to your career. This milestone asks your group to invite them.

## What to do

At least one member of your group publishes a **LinkedIn post** inviting their professional and personal network to your poster presentation at the Spring Undergraduate Research Conference. The post includes, **exactly as listed in the official conference program**:

- the poster title;
- the team members;
- the date and time of your presentation;
- the location (building and room or space).

Write in a professional tone for a public audience, say that you are presenting at Purdue University's Spring Undergraduate Research Conference, and encourage your network to come. You may add a visual (a team photo, a project logo, a graphic from the poster), and you may tag Professor Moreira: <https://www.linkedin.com/in/davimoreira/>.

An example (you do not have to follow it):

> I am excited to share that my QM47400 Predictive Analytics team, mentored by Prof. Moreira, will present our research project at Purdue University's **Spring Undergraduate Research Conference**!
>
> **Poster title:** [your project title] · **Team members:** [every member's full name] · **Date and time:** [from the conference program] · **Location:** [building and room or space]
>
> Our project explores [one or two sentences on the focus and its impact]. We would be delighted if you could join us. \#Purdue \#DanielsSchoolofBusiness \#UndergraduateResearch \#DataScience \#PredictiveAnalytics \#BoilerUp

## What to submit

One member uploads `NN_linkedin.pdf` for the whole group: a screenshot of the published post, saved as a PDF.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: a screenshot, by the deadline, of a published post that contains the title, the team members, the date and time, and the location.

## Tips

- Wait for the official program, then copy the title, the time and the location from it exactly.
- Agree in the group on who posts, so the post is up well before the due date.
- Read the post once as a stranger would: would you know where to go, and when?

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
