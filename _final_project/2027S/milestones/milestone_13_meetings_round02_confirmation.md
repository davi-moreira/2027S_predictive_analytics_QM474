## What this milestone is for

This milestone closes the loop you opened in Milestone 07. There your group emailed the TA and Professor Moreira to propose meeting times; here you **present your project at both Round 02 meetings** and confirm that the meetings took place.

## What to do

Your two Round 02 meetings were:

- the **TA meeting**, between Monday, March 8 and Sunday, March 14, or between Monday, March 22 and Sunday, March 28;
- the **meeting with Professor Moreira**, between Monday, March 29 and Saturday, April 3.

These meetings focus on your predictive models, your modeling strategy and your poster. **Every member attends at least one of the two meetings and presents part of the deck**; it does not have to be the same people both times.

**Every meeting starts with your group's presentation.** Prepare about five slides and five minutes:
(1) the stakeholder and the decision your predictions support; (2) your data; (3) what you did since the
last meeting; (4) your current results, in the course's terms; (5) the two or three questions you want
help with. Update the deck between the TA meeting and the meeting with Professor Moreira, so the second
one shows what changed after the first.

In this round, "current results" means your champion model and how you chose it: the margin you declared before comparing, the paired comparison on the same five folds, and the outcome. At the meeting with Professor Moreira, add one slide with your poster draft.

Upload the slides to this milestone's Brightspace folder **before** each meeting: `NN_round02_ta_slides.pdf` before the TA meeting and `NN_round02_instructor_slides.pdf` before the meeting with Professor Moreira. The folder opens on Monday, March 8 and accepts several uploads.

After both meetings, make one short PDF that lists, for each of the two meetings, whether it was with the TA or with Professor Moreira, its date and time, who from your group attended and who presented.

## What to submit

One member uploads, for the whole group, the two slide decks (each before its meeting) and `NN_meetings_round02.pdf` (both meetings in the same file), for example `03_round02_ta_slides.pdf`, `03_round02_instructor_slides.pdf` and `03_meetings_round02.pdf`.

## How it is graded

This milestone is graded for completion: both slide decks, each uploaded before its meeting and presented there, and the confirmation PDF by the deadline, with the date, the time, the attendees and the presenters of both meetings. **A meeting held without a presentation does not count**, and a member who presents at neither meeting does not receive this milestone's credit.

## Tips

- Write down the date, the time and who attended right after each meeting.
- If different members went to each meeting, say so.

*The Final Project Overview PDF has every date, the meeting rounds, and how the project is graded.*
