## What this milestone is for

Your group reviews the final posters of five other groups and gives them specific, constructive feedback, the kind that helps someone improve their research and the way they present it. Reviewing other people's work critically is also one of the fastest ways to see your own poster clearly.

Peer *review* is not peer *evaluation*. Here your group reviews **other groups' posters**. Peer evaluation, due Friday, April 30, is where each of you confidentially rates **your own teammates' contributions**. Both count, separately.

## What to do

1. **Find your posters.** The five groups whose posters you review are listed on Brightspace, and the poster PDFs are in a shared folder linked there.
2. **Review each poster carefully.** Look at the research question, the methods, the findings and the visual design, and take notes as you go; they make the survey faster and better.
3. **Fill in the Poster Peer Review Survey** (linked on Brightspace) **once per poster**, five surveys in all. Write constructive comments wherever the survey asks for them, and take a screenshot of each submission confirmation.
4. **Put the five screenshots in one PDF** and upload it.

## What to submit

One member uploads a single PDF for the whole group with the five confirmation screenshots, one per survey. Complete every survey before the deadline.

## How it is graded

Peer review is worth 10% of your Final Project grade. Submit all five reviews, each with comments that are specific and useful to the group that receives them.

## Tips

- **Be specific.** Instead of "I didn't like the plots", try "It would help to label the x-axis of the plot about …".
- **Stay constructive.** Point toward the fix: "Consider adding a short paragraph explaining your data cleaning."
- **Balance the positive and the negative.** Say what works as well as what could be better.
