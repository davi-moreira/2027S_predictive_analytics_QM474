## What this milestone is for

After the conference, your team spends the last weeks of the term working like a product team. You turn your validated model into something a business decision-maker can use: **(1) an interactive dashboard, (2) a one-page brief and (3) a three-minute video**, built with an AI assistant and checked against your own evidence. You leave with a product you can show an employer, real feedback, and practice in checking AI-built work. ## What to do

1. **Friday, April 16 (class): kickoff.** Open your team's AI workspace and finish this sentence together: *"Our dashboard helps \_\_\_ decide \_\_\_."*
2. **Before class on Monday, April 19: export your evidence.** Paste the export cell (on Brightspace) at the end of your final notebook, point its first lines at your own model, data, cross-validation splitter and metric, and run it. **Check that the cross-validation number it prints matches your poster.** Upload the two files it saves, with your poster PDF, to your team's AI workspace.
3. **Monday, April 19 (class): build.** Ask the assistant for the dashboard, for example: *"Build a dashboard as ONE self-contained HTML file (all data and code inside, no internet needed) that helps [who] decide [what]. Use only the numbers in the attached files. Add [one filter] and [one comparison]. Show our cross-validation result and our limitations. Describe top features as what the model relies on, not as causes. Do not invent data."*
4. **Wednesday, April 21 (class): test it.** Download the HTML and open it on a different computer. Click every control, then check three numbers against your notebook.
5. **Friday, April 23 (class): the classmate check.** Give two classmates from another team one minute each. Can they find (a) the decision, (b) the key number and (c) one limit? Fix what they missed.
6. **Monday, April 26, 11:59 PM: submit,** one submission per team: `dashboard.html`; `brief.pdf` (use the template on Brightspace); `poster.pdf` (your final conference poster); and `video_link.txt`, an unlisted link to your three-minute video. The video follows the storyboard decision, demo, key number, one limit; turn on captions and check that the link plays when you are signed out. Change nothing after this deadline.
7. **Wednesday, April 28 (class): showcase and an 8-minute team debrief.** At your station, your team answers five short questions: Who decides what with your product? How good is your model, and how do you know? What should a manager *not* conclude? How did you check something the AI built? What would you fix first?
8. **Wednesday, April 28, 11:59 PM, on your own: your live dashboard link.** Publish your team's final `dashboard.html`, unchanged, under your own account, and submit on Brightspace the link plus one sentence for your résumé or LinkedIn: *"This dashboard helps [who] decide [what], using [evidence]."* With GitHub Pages it takes about ten minutes: create a free account and a new **public** repository; upload `dashboard.html` renamed to `index.html`; go to Settings, then Pages, and deploy from the branch `main`; your link is `https://<username>.github.io/<repository>/`. Open it on your phone, logged out, before you submit. Publish only if your data are public; otherwise ask Professor Moreira before Monday, April 26.
9. **Friday, April 30: peer evaluation** (due 11:59 PM) and the end-of-course reflection survey.

## What to submit

As a **team**, one Brightspace submission by Monday, April 26: the four files above. **Individually**, by Wednesday, April 28: your live link and its one-sentence description.

## How it is graded

Poster-to-Product is 5% of the course grade, scored out of 100 points:

- **48, product (team):** reviewers' average ratings of your product on the judging rubric (attached on Brightspace), which has four criteria worth 12 points each: decision usefulness, clarity and ease of use, evidence and honesty, and communication. Professor Moreira reviews every product and assigns the final grade. [[PENDING p2p-funding: who reviews the products if there are no outside reviewers]]
- **12, team debrief** at the showcase.
- **20, peer evaluation** of your sprint team.
- **20, your live dashboard link** (individual, graded by the TAs).

You will receive all the reviewers' comments.

## Rules and tips

- Use only your project's real data and results.
- Do not open or re-test your test set: it was opened once, for your champion, in Milestone 08.
- Do not upload personal or private data to any AI tool.
- Stuck? Ask your TA.
